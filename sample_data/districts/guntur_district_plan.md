# Guntur District Development Plan

FICTIONAL SAMPLE DOCUMENT. Prepared for a software engineering assessment. The Sunrise Coast Alliance (SCA), its schemes and all figures are invented.

## 1. About This Plan

This plan lists the Sunrise Coast Alliance's commitments for Guntur district, including Guntur city, Tenali, Narasaraopet and the surrounding rural mandals. Guntur is one of India's largest chilli-producing and trading regions, so agriculture is the centre of this plan.

## 2. Agriculture in Guntur

### 2.1 Chilli Cold Storage

A 1 lakh tonne chilli cold storage complex will be built near the Guntur chilli market yard, so that farmers can store produce and avoid distress sales when prices crash after harvest.

### 2.2 Chilli Price Stabilisation

Under the statewide Kisan Price Guarantee Fund, a dedicated price stabilisation window will purchase chilli whenever the market price falls below 15,000 rupees per quintal.

### 2.3 Crop Insurance and Advice

Chilli and cotton farmers will receive free soil testing and weather-linked crop insurance with claims settled within 45 days.

## 3. Healthcare in Guntur

A 150-bed cancer care block with radiotherapy will be added to the Guntur Government General Hospital. Fifty additional doctors will be posted to primary health centres in rural Guntur. Tenali Area Hospital will be upgraded with a 20-bed intensive care unit.

## 4. Education in Guntur

Government engineering and polytechnic colleges in Guntur will receive laboratory upgrade grants of 2 crore rupees each. A residential school for girls will be opened in every assembly constituency of the district.

## 5. Employment in Guntur

A food processing park for chilli, cotton and turmeric products at Guntur is expected to create 6,500 jobs. The Yuva Naipunya Skill Hub in Guntur will focus on food processing, warehousing and agri-machinery repair.

## 6. Drinking Water

A drinking water pipeline from the Krishna will supply 135 litres per person per day to Guntur city and Tenali by 2028.
