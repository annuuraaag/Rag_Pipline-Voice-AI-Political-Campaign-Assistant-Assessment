# Nellore Coastal Livelihoods Plan

FICTIONAL SAMPLE DOCUMENT. Prepared for a software engineering assessment demo. The Sunrise Coast Alliance (SCA), its schemes and all figures are invented.

## 1. About This Plan

This plan lists the Sunrise Coast Alliance's commitments for Nellore district, covering Nellore city, Kavali, Gudur and the coastal mandals. Fishing, aquaculture and paddy farming are the main livelihoods, so the plan is built around them.

## 2. Fishermen and Aquaculture

### 2.1 Samudra Suraksha Scheme

Under the new Samudra Suraksha Scheme, every registered fishing family in Nellore will receive 12,000 rupees during the 61-day annual fishing ban, paid in two instalments directly into the family's bank account.

### 2.2 Krishnapatnam Fishing Harbour

A modern fishing harbour with berths for 400 boats, an ice plant and a fish auction hall will be built at Krishnapatnam. Construction will begin within 18 months of the government taking office.

### 2.3 Shrimp Farmers

Aqua farmers will get electricity at 1.50 rupees per unit, and a disease testing laboratory for shrimp will be opened in Gudur.

## 3. Healthcare in Nellore

A 200-bed mother and child hospital will be built in Nellore city. Every coastal mandal will get a boat ambulance service for island villages, and a mobile dialysis unit will visit Kavali and Gudur every week.

## 4. Water and Cyclone Safety

Thirty new multi-purpose cyclone shelters will be built along the Nellore coast, each with solar power, a kitchen and space for 500 people. Pulicat lake villages will receive a 95 crore rupee drinking water project.

## 5. Youth and Jobs

A seafood processing and export park at Krishnapatnam is expected to create 8,000 jobs. The Yuva Naipunya Skill Hub in Nellore will offer courses in marine engineering, cold chain logistics and boat engine repair.
