SCA Farmers Charter

FICTIONAL SAMPLE DOCUMENT. Prepared for a software engineering assessment. All schemes and figures are invented.

1. Purpose

This charter sets out the Sunrise Coast Alliance's commitments to farmers, farm workers and fishermen across Andhra Pradesh.

2. Price Support

The Kisan Price Guarantee Fund of 3,000 crore rupees will purchase chilli, cotton, paddy and maize whenever market prices fall below the support price announced before each sowing season. Payments will reach farmers' bank accounts within seven days of procurement.

3. Input Support

Small and marginal farmers owning up to five acres will receive 12,000 rupees per acre per year, up to a maximum of five acres, to cover seeds and fertiliser. Tenant farmers with a cultivator rights card will also be eligible.

4. Irrigation and Storage

Pending minor irrigation projects covering 3 lakh acres will be completed within three years. Cold storage capacity of 5 lakh tonnes will be built across the state, including the 1 lakh tonne chilli cold storage complex in Guntur.

5. Crop Insurance

Weather-linked crop insurance will be free for farmers with up to five acres, and claims will be settled within 45 days of a crop loss assessment.

6. Farm Workers and Fishermen

Landless agricultural workers will receive accident insurance of 5 lakh rupees. Registered fishermen will receive a lean-season allowance of 10,000 rupees per family during the annual fishing ban.
