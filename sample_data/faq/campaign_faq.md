# Sunrise Coast Alliance Campaign FAQ

FICTIONAL SAMPLE DOCUMENT. Prepared for a software engineering assessment. All names and details are invented.

## 1. What is the Sunrise Coast Alliance?

The Sunrise Coast Alliance (SCA) is a political alliance contesting the 2026 Andhra Pradesh Assembly elections. Its programme is described in the People's Manifesto 2026 and in district development plans for Vijayawada, Guntur and Visakhapatnam.

## 2. Who is the candidate for Vijayawada Central?

Dr. Anitha Rao Kotagiri, a paediatrician and the Alliance's state spokesperson on health, is the candidate for Vijayawada Central.

## 3. How do I check if I am registered to vote?

Voter registration is managed by the Election Commission of India, not by any party. You can check your registration on the official Election Commission voter services portal or by contacting your Booth Level Officer. Polling dates are announced only by the Election Commission.

## 4. Are the schemes in the manifesto already in effect?

No. The schemes described in the manifesto are proposals that the Alliance says it will implement if elected. They are not current government schemes.

## 5. How will the promises be funded?

The manifesto states that programmes will be funded within the state budget through improved tax collection and reduced wasteful expenditure, without new taxes on essential goods. Each commitment lists an estimated annual cost.

## 6. How can I volunteer?

You can register as a volunteer at any SCA campaign office or by calling the volunteer helpline at 1800-000-2026, available from 9 AM to 9 PM.

## 7. Where is the Vijayawada campaign office?

The Vijayawada Central campaign office is at 14 Eluru Road, Governorpet, Vijayawada, open from 10 AM to 6 PM, Monday to Saturday.

This assistant can only answer from the published campaign documents. If a topic is not covered in those documents, it will say so rather than guess.
