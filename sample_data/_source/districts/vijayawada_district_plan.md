# Vijayawada District Development Plan

FICTIONAL SAMPLE DOCUMENT. Prepared for a software engineering assessment. The Sunrise Coast Alliance (SCA), its schemes and all figures are invented.

## 1. About This Plan

This plan describes the Sunrise Coast Alliance's specific commitments for Vijayawada city and the surrounding NTR district. It complements the statewide People's Manifesto 2026. Vijayawada Central is contested for the Alliance by Dr. Anitha Rao Kotagiri.

## 2. Healthcare in Vijayawada

### 2.1 Government General Hospital

A new 200-bed mother and child health block will be built at Government General Hospital, Vijayawada, including a 60-bed neonatal intensive care unit. Construction is planned to start within 12 months, with an estimated cost of 240 crore rupees.

### 2.2 Urban Primary Health Centres

Twelve urban primary health centres in Vijayawada, including those in Ajit Singh Nagar, Krishna Lanka and Patamata, will be upgraded to run 24 hours a day with an emergency doctor on duty. Twenty-eight new Arogya Mitra ward clinics will be opened in wards that currently have no government clinic.

### 2.3 Dialysis and Mobile Clinics

A 30-machine free dialysis centre will be established at Gollapudi. Two mobile clinic vans will visit riverbank colonies along the Krishna and the Budameru every week, offering check-ups, maternal care and free medicines.

## 3. Education in Vijayawada

All 186 government schools in Vijayawada will receive digital classrooms and science laboratories under Vidya Vikas within two years. A new government degree college for women will be established in Vijayawada East with 1,500 seats. Students of government colleges in the city will receive free bus passes on city buses.

## 4. Jobs and Skills in Vijayawada

A Vijayawada logistics and warehousing park near the national highway is expected to create 8,000 jobs. A Yuva Naipunya Skill Hub at Gunadala will train 6,000 young people per year in logistics, retail, electrical work and nursing assistance.

## 5. Flood Protection and Infrastructure

The floods of recent years showed that large parts of the city are vulnerable to overflow from the Budameru canal. The Alliance commits to widening and lining 18 kilometres of the Budameru canal, building a retaining wall along the Krishna river front, and implementing a 1,200 crore rupee storm-water drainage master plan. A flood early-warning system with SMS alerts will cover every low-lying colony.

## 6. Transport

A detailed project report for a Vijayawada light-metro connecting the bus station, the railway station and the airport will be completed within the first year. One hundred electric buses will be added to city routes.
