# SCA Healthcare Schemes Guide

FICTIONAL SAMPLE DOCUMENT. Prepared for a software engineering assessment. All schemes and figures are invented.

This guide explains who is eligible for each healthcare scheme proposed by the Sunrise Coast Alliance and how to apply. The schemes apply statewide, including Vijayawada, Guntur and Visakhapatnam.

## 1. SCA Health Shield

**What it offers:** cashless treatment worth up to 15 lakh rupees per family per year for 2,400 procedures, including cancer treatment, cardiac surgery, dialysis, knee replacement and organ transplants.

**Who is eligible:** families with an annual income below 8 lakh rupees. Families already covered by a central government health insurance scheme receive the Health Shield as a top-up.

**How to enrol:** take your ration card and Aadhaar card to the nearest village or ward secretariat. A Health Shield card is issued within 15 days. No premium is charged.

## 2. Arogya Mitra Ward Clinics

Arogya Mitra clinics will offer free doctor consultations, 120 free diagnostic tests and free generic medicines from 8 AM to 8 PM every day. No registration or income proof is needed. Each clinic serves roughly 15,000 residents.

## 3. Jeevan Dhara Free Medicines

All essential generic medicines on the state list will be free at government hospitals, Arogya Mitra clinics and primary health centres. Patients with chronic conditions such as diabetes and hypertension will receive a 30-day supply at each visit.

## 4. Matru Poshana Nutrition Kit

Pregnant women who register at any government health facility receive a monthly nutrition kit with protein supplements, iron and folic acid tablets, millets and pulses. The kit continues for six months after delivery.

## 5. Rapid Response Ambulance

The Alliance commits to an ambulance response time of 15 minutes in cities and 30 minutes in rural areas, with 1,000 new ambulances equipped for basic life support.

## 6. Frequently Confused Points

The Health Shield is an insurance scheme for hospital treatment; Arogya Mitra clinics provide everyday outpatient care. A family can use both. Private hospital treatment is covered only at hospitals empanelled under the Health Shield.
