# SCA Education Schemes Guide

FICTIONAL SAMPLE DOCUMENT. Prepared for a software engineering assessment. All schemes and figures are invented.

This guide explains the education schemes proposed by the Sunrise Coast Alliance, who can apply and what each scheme provides. All schemes apply across the state.

## 1. Pratibha Scholarship

The Pratibha Scholarship provides 20,000 rupees per year to degree and diploma students, and 35,000 rupees per year to engineering and medical students. Students are eligible if their family income is below 5 lakh rupees per year and they attend at least 75 percent of classes. Applications open every July on the Citizen First portal. The scholarship is paid directly into the student's bank account in two instalments.

## 2. Vidya Vikas School Upgrade

Vidya Vikas will upgrade every government school with separate toilets for girls, safe drinking water, a science laboratory, a library and at least two digital classrooms. Schools will be upgraded in four phases, starting with schools that have the lowest facilities scores.

## 3. Udayam Breakfast

Students from Class 1 to Class 10 in government and government-aided schools will receive a free hot breakfast every school day. The menu will include millets, eggs or bananas, and will be prepared by local women's self-help groups.

## 4. Free Bus Pass for Students

Students of government schools and colleges will travel free on state transport buses between home and their place of study. Girl students will receive the free pass up to postgraduate level.

## 5. Teacher Recruitment

The Alliance will hold an annual teacher recruitment examination and fill 18,000 vacant government teacher posts within two years. Priority will be given to mathematics, science and English teachers in rural schools.
