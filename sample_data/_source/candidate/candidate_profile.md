# Candidate Profile: Dr. Anitha Rao Kotagiri

## 1. Background

Dr. Anitha Rao Kotagiri is the Sunrise Coast Alliance candidate for Vijayawada Central. She is a paediatrician who worked for 18 years in the paediatrics department of Government General Hospital, Vijayawada. She holds an MBBS and an MD in Paediatrics.

In 2012 she founded Chinnari Care, a volunteer network that runs free weekend children's health camps in low-income colonies of Vijayawada. She joined the Sunrise Coast Alliance in 2019 and serves as the Alliance's state spokesperson on health.

## 2. Priorities

Dr. Kotagiri has named three priorities for Vijayawada Central: maternal and child health, flood-resilient neighbourhoods, and girls' education.

## 3. Healthcare Commitments for Vijayawada Central

- Complete the 200-bed mother and child health block at Government General Hospital, Vijayawada, including the 60-bed neonatal intensive care unit.
- Ensure that every child in government schools in Vijayawada Central receives a health check-up twice a year, with free spectacles and dental care where needed.
- Expand Chinnari Care camps into a permanent government-supported programme in every ward of Vijayawada Central.
- Hold a monthly public hearing on hospital services where patients can report problems directly.

## 4. Other Commitments

She has committed to publishing a quarterly report on the progress of the Budameru canal and drainage works, and to setting up a scholarship help desk in her constituency office for students applying for the Pratibha Scholarship.

## 5. Constituency Office

The Vijayawada Central campaign office is located at 14 Eluru Road, Governorpet, Vijayawada. It is open from 10 AM to 6 PM, Monday to Saturday.
