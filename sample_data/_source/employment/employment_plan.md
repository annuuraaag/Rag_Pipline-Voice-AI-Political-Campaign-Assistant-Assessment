# SCA Youth Employment and Skills Plan

FICTIONAL SAMPLE DOCUMENT. Prepared for a software engineering assessment. All schemes and figures are invented.

## 1. The Problem

Youth unemployment is one of the main concerns raised during the Alliance's public consultations. This plan explains how the Sunrise Coast Alliance proposes to create jobs and improve skills for young people.

## 2. Yuva Naipunya Skill Hubs

A Yuva Naipunya Skill Hub will operate in every district headquarters. Courses last three to six months and are free. Trainees receive a monthly allowance of 2,000 rupees during training. Each hub works with local employers so that at least 60 percent of trainees are placed in jobs within six months of completing a course.

## 3. Apprenticeship Stipend

Graduates and diploma holders who join a registered apprenticeship receive 6,000 rupees per month for up to 12 months. The state will pay half the stipend for apprentices hired by MSMEs.

## 4. MSME Credit Guarantee Fund

A 5,000 crore rupee credit guarantee fund will let micro, small and medium enterprises borrow up to 50 lakh rupees without collateral. First-generation entrepreneurs from SC, ST and BC communities and women entrepreneurs will receive a 3 percent interest subsidy.

## 6. Government Recruitment

All vacant government posts will be filled through an annual job calendar published every January, with examinations conducted on fixed dates.
