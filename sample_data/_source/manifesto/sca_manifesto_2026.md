# Sunrise Coast Alliance People's Manifesto 2026

FICTIONAL SAMPLE DOCUMENT. This manifesto was written for a software engineering assessment. The Sunrise Coast Alliance (SCA), its candidates, schemes and all figures are invented and do not describe any real party or government programme.

## 1. Our Vision for Andhra Pradesh

The Sunrise Coast Alliance presents this manifesto for the 2026 Andhra Pradesh Assembly elections. It sets out commitments in seven areas: healthcare, education, employment, agriculture, water and urban infrastructure, women and social welfare, and governance. District-specific commitments for Vijayawada, Guntur and Visakhapatnam are listed in Section 9.

Every commitment in this document carries a delivery timeline and an estimated annual cost, so that citizens can track progress. The Alliance states that all programmes will be funded within the state budget without new taxes on essential goods.

## 2. Healthcare

The Alliance proposes to raise state health spending from an estimated 6 percent to 8 percent of the state budget by 2029.

### 2.1 Arogya Mitra Ward Clinics

The Alliance will open an Arogya Mitra clinic in every urban ward and every village panchayat cluster within three years. Each clinic will be staffed by a doctor, two nurses and a pharmacist, will be open from 8 AM to 8 PM, and will offer free consultations and 120 free diagnostic tests. The estimated cost is 1,900 crore rupees per year.

### 2.2 SCA Health Shield

The SCA Health Shield is a cashless health insurance scheme covering up to 15 lakh rupees per family per year. Families with an annual income below 8 lakh rupees will be eligible. The scheme will cover 2,400 medical procedures, including cancer treatment, cardiac surgery, dialysis and organ transplants, at government and empanelled private hospitals.

### 2.3 Free Medicines and Diagnostics

Under the Jeevan Dhara programme, all essential generic medicines will be supplied free at government hospitals and Arogya Mitra clinics. Every district hospital will receive an MRI and CT scanning facility by 2028.

### 2.4 Maternal and Child Health

Pregnant women registered at a government facility will receive a Matru Poshana nutrition kit every month during pregnancy and for six months after delivery. The Alliance will add 600 neonatal intensive care beds across the state's district hospitals.

## 3. Education

### 3.1 Vidya Vikas School Upgrade

The Vidya Vikas programme will upgrade all 44,000 government schools with clean toilets, drinking water, science laboratories and digital classrooms over four years, at an estimated cost of 3,500 crore rupees per year.

### 3.2 Pratibha Scholarship

The Pratibha Scholarship will provide 20,000 rupees per year to every degree and diploma student whose family income is below 5 lakh rupees per year. Students in engineering and medical courses will receive 35,000 rupees per year.

### 3.3 Teachers

The Alliance will fill 18,000 vacant government teacher posts within two years through an annual, transparent recruitment examination.

### 3.4 Udayam Breakfast

Government school students from Class 1 to Class 10 will receive a free hot breakfast every school day, in addition to the existing midday meal.

<!-- pagebreak -->

## 4. Employment and Skills

### 4.1 Yuva Naipunya Skill Hubs

A Yuva Naipunya Skill Hub will be set up in every district headquarters to train 2 lakh young people per year in trades such as electrical work, logistics, nursing assistance, welding and software testing, with placement support.

### 4.2 MSME Credit Guarantee Fund

A 5,000 crore rupee credit guarantee fund will allow micro, small and medium enterprises to borrow up to 50 lakh rupees without collateral.

### 4.3 Apprenticeship Stipend

Graduates enrolled in a registered apprenticeship will receive a stipend of 6,000 rupees per month for up to 12 months.

## 5. Agriculture

### 5.1 Kisan Price Guarantee Fund

A 3,000 crore rupee Kisan Price Guarantee Fund will buy chilli, cotton, paddy and maize from farmers whenever market prices fall below the announced support price.

### 5.2 Cold Storage and Irrigation

The Alliance will build cold storage capacity of 5 lakh tonnes across the state and complete pending minor irrigation projects covering 3 lakh acres.

### 5.3 Fisheries

Registered fishermen will receive a lean-season allowance of 10,000 rupees per family during the annual fishing ban period.

<!-- pagebreak -->

## 6. Water and Urban Infrastructure

### 6.1 Drinking Water

Every household will receive a piped drinking water connection by 2029 under the Jal Samruddhi mission.

### 6.2 Flood Protection

The Alliance will prepare flood management master plans for cities on the Krishna and Godavari rivers, with early-warning systems and storm-water drains. Details for Vijayawada are given in Section 9.1.

### 6.3 Roads and Transport

All rural habitations will be connected by all-weather roads, and 2,000 new electric buses will be added to the state transport fleet.

## 7. Women and Social Welfare

Old-age, widow and disability pensions will be raised to 4,000 rupees per month. Women's self-help groups will be eligible for interest-free loans of up to 10 lakh rupees. A women's safety helpline with a guaranteed 15-minute police response will operate in all cities.

## 8. Governance

A Citizen First portal will guarantee delivery of 300 government services within 30 days, with automatic compensation for delays. All government tenders above 1 crore rupees will be published online, and an independent social audit will review every welfare scheme each year.

## 9. District Commitments

### 9.1 Vijayawada

In Vijayawada, the Alliance commits to widening and lining the Budameru canal, building a retaining wall along the Krishna river front, and a 1,200 crore rupee storm-water drainage master plan to prevent urban flooding. Government General Hospital, Vijayawada will receive a 200-bed mother and child health block. A detailed project report for a Vijayawada light-metro will be completed in the first year.

### 9.2 Guntur

In Guntur, the Alliance commits to a 1 lakh tonne chilli cold storage complex, a chilli price stabilisation mechanism under the Kisan Price Guarantee Fund, and a 150-bed cancer care block at the Guntur Government General Hospital.

### 9.3 Visakhapatnam

In Visakhapatnam, the Alliance commits to an IT and electronics corridor expected to create 25,000 jobs, modernisation of the fishing harbour, and continuous air-quality monitoring around industrial zones.
