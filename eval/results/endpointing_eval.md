Generated by `eval/run_endpointing_eval.py`; do not edit by hand. 32 questions from `eval/queries.jsonl`, as a browser recogniser delivers them (lower case, no punctuation), assessed word by word with their conversation history.

Waits: complete 300 ms · likely 550 ms · unsure 900 ms · incomplete 1600 ms. Baseline: a fixed 900 ms after the last word.

Verdicts mid-question: {'incomplete': 110, 'unsure': 38, 'likely': 42, 'complete': 9}. **55%** of mid-question points (after 'for', 'the', 'um', …) now wait longer than the fixed 900 ms, so a hesitation there no longer cuts the question off.

Reading the trade-off: a finished question is answered sooner, and pauses after words that need a continuation are safer than before. The cost: a pause longer than 300–550 ms right after a phrase that already sounds complete ("…propose", "…for Guntur" before "and Visakhapatnam") now ends the turn. The design bets that people hesitate after "for", "the" or "um" more often than after a complete phrase; real usage logs should confirm it. Turning the setting off restores the fixed pause.
